Summer camp instructors lead a week of introductory robotics activities
for middle-school students, covering basic mechanical assembly, simple
programming, and teamwork exercises. No prior teaching experience is
required; a short orientation session is provided beforehand.

**Responsibilities**

- Lead daily activities from a prepared curriculum
- Support small groups of students in assembling and programming a kit
  robot
- Help manage classroom logistics alongside a co-instructor
- Complete a brief written debrief at the end of the week

**Qualifications**

- Comfort working with middle-school-aged students
- Basic familiarity with robotics or programming concepts
- Availability for the full camp week, including a half-day orientation
