Outreach volunteers represent the organization at community events —
school visits, STEM fairs, and regional competitions — helping run
demonstrations and answer questions from students and families about
robotics and the organization's programs.

**Responsibilities**

- Staff a demonstration table at community and school events
- Run simple, prepared activities for visitors of varying ages
- Answer general questions about the organization and its programs
- Help transport and set up demonstration equipment

**Qualifications**

- Comfort speaking with the public, including younger students
- Reliable transportation to event locations
- No robotics background required; a short orientation is provided
