Build team volunteers work alongside students in the shop, helping design,
fabricate, and test the robot each season. This is a hands-on volunteer
role, not a paid position: the organization runs on the time its
volunteers contribute.

**Responsibilities**

- Support students in the design and machining of mechanical subsystems
- Help maintain shop tools and enforce safe workshop practices
- Review student CAD work and offer engineering feedback
- Attend weekly build sessions during the competition season

**Qualifications**

- Background in mechanical or manufacturing engineering, or equivalent
  hands-on experience
- Comfort working with students without doing the work for them
- Availability for evening or weekend build sessions during the season
