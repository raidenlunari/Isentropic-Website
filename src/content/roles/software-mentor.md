Software mentors meet regularly with student programming teams to review
code, discuss design decisions, and help debug issues that come up during
the season. The role is remote and scheduled around the mentor's
availability.

**Responsibilities**

- Hold a weekly check-in with one or two student teams
- Review pull requests and offer feedback on structure and readability
- Help students work through debugging problems rather than solving them
  outright
- Suggest resources for topics the students want to learn on their own

**Qualifications**

- Professional or academic experience in software engineering
- Familiarity with version control and collaborative code review
- Patience for working with students who are still learning the basics
