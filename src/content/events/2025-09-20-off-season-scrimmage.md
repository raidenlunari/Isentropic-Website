---
title: "Off-Season Scrimmage"
date: 2025-09-20
location: "Sunset High School gymnasium, Beaverton, OR"
summary: "Isentropic Robotics hosted an off-season scrimmage to test a redesigned drivetrain and onboard new members before the coming build season."
---

Roughly thirty students attended, split evenly between returning members and
newcomers recruited over the summer. Three neighboring teams accepted the
invitation and brought robots from the prior season, giving the group enough
robots on the field to run full six-team matches for most of the afternoon.

The scrimmage was organized jointly with the Sunset High School robotics
club, which supplied the gymnasium and field elements left over from a
regional event earlier in the year. A local hardware distributor donated a
set of practice batteries for the day, which meant no team had to sit out a
match waiting for a charge.

The redesigned swerve drivetrain performed well enough to confirm the
mechanical direction for the next build season, though the scouting team
flagged a wiring layout that made mid-match repairs slower than intended.
That note was folded directly into the season's build checklist. New members
rotated through drive, scouting, and pit crew roles throughout the day, and
several asked to join the mechanical subteam permanently afterward.
