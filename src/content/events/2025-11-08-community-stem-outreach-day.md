---
title: "Community STEM Outreach Day"
date: 2025-11-08
location: "Beaverton City Library"
summary: "Team members ran hands-on robotics demonstrations for elementary and middle school students in partnership with the Beaverton City Library and a regional STEM nonprofit."
---

The event drew around 120 attendees over the course of the morning, most of
them elementary and middle school students accompanied by a parent or
guardian. Isentropic Robotics staffed four stations: a driving course for the
prior season's competition robot, a soldering table supervised by two
mentors, a LEGO-based mechanism-building activity for younger children, and a
question table where alumni described what a season on the team actually
involves.

The library provided the meeting room and handled registration, while
Northwest STEM Partners, a regional nonprofit that funds outreach at several
area schools, covered the cost of take-home electronics kits for every child
who visited the soldering table. This was the team's second year running the
event with both partners, and attendance grew by roughly a third over the
previous year.

Several families asked about joining a beginner-friendly program, which the
team does not currently offer below high school age; that gap has been
raised with Northwest STEM Partners as a possible joint project for next
year. In the meantime, two of the visiting middle schoolers' families
followed up to ask about touring the team's workshop, and both tours have
since been scheduled.
