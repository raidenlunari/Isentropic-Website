---
title: "Cascade Regional Qualifier"
date: 2026-03-14
location: "Roosevelt High School, Portland, OR"
summary: "Isentropic Robotics competed in its first regional qualifier of the season, finishing sixth in qualification matches and advancing to the alliance selection round."
---

The team brought twenty-two students and four mentors to the two-day event,
along with a rotating pit crew that kept the robot on the field for all ten
of its qualification matches. The drive team finished qualifications ranked
sixth of thirty-six teams and was selected as the second pick for the
third-seeded alliance, which advanced to the semifinals before being
eliminated.

Cascade Precision, the team's machining sponsor, sent a representative to
staff the pit and assist with an overnight repair after a mechanism jammed
during Friday's final qualification match; the part was remachined and
reinstalled before Saturday's matches began. The team also shared pit space
with a first-year team from a neighboring school, an informal mentorship
arrangement that the two teams' lead mentors have agreed to continue at the
next event.

The scouting team's match-prediction accuracy, tracked as an internal metric
since last season, held at just under eighty percent across the event, which
the strategy lead credits to a revised scouting form introduced during
preseason. That form will carry forward to the team's second regional next
month unchanged.
