---
title: "Build Season Kickoff Workshop"
date: 2026-01-17
location: "Isentropic Robotics workshop, Beaverton, OR"
summary: "The team opened the new build season with a full-day workshop covering the game rules, CAD conventions, and shop safety before subteams began prototyping."
---

Attendance was near-total for the first weekend of the season: forty-one of
the team's forty-six registered members were present, along with five
mentors. The morning was spent watching the season's game reveal as a group
and working through the rulebook section by section, and the afternoon split
into subteam sessions on CAD conventions, machine shop safety, and the
programming team's software stack.

A mechanical engineer from Cascade Precision, the team's longtime machining
sponsor, volunteered for the shop safety session and stayed through the
afternoon to answer questions from students using the mill and lathe for the
first time. Two alumni now studying engineering at nearby universities
returned to help run the CAD session for newer members.

By the end of the day, each subteam had a rough strategy brief and a list of
mechanisms to prototype in the first week, and the shop's tool checkout log
showed every major tool signed out at least once, a reasonable proxy for
how much of the membership actually got hands-on. The safety session
uncovered that several returning members had never been checked off on the
belt sander, which has since been added as a standing item in the season's
first-week checklist.
