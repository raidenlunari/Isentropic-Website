The camp ran in two sessions: the first from June 23 to July 3, and the
second from July 6 to July 17. Instruction was organized around three
areas: competitive robotics fundamentals, robot documentation, and robot
programming.

**By the numbers**

- 13 attendees
- $7,500 in program revenue
